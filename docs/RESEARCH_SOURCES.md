# Research sources

- **SEC EDGAR** — 10-K / 10-Q / 8-K and company facts; respect user-agent, caching, and rate limits. Cite accession, form, dates, URLs, and facts.
- **Broker / licensed market data** — prices, optionability, yields, ex-dividend calendars (SEC alone is insufficient).
- Distinguish **declared** vs forecast dividends. Never fabricate absent data.
- LLM memos require typed claims + citations; validate independently; treat retrieved text as untrusted (prompt-injection resistant). Research agents have **no** order credentials.
